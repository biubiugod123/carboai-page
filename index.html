Questions

Is Carbo-AI free?

Carbo-AI is free to download. Using it requires a Carbo-AI Pro subscription, billed through the App Store. If a free trial is offered, its terms are shown before you buy. Cancel anytime.

How accurate is the carb estimate?

It's an estimate: the app recognises the food, estimates the portion and looks up its nutrition. Treat it as a starting point and edit it before you save when it's off. How the estimate works.

What happens to my photos?

Your photo goes to our servers and on to a third-party AI provider to recognise the food. Once the estimate comes back it's discarded — we don't store meal photos on our servers. The photo stays on your phone with the saved meal.

Does it work on Android?

Not yet — Carbo-AI is iPhone-only.

How do I cancel?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Carbo-AI → Cancel. Deleting the app doesn't cancel the subscription.

How do I delete my account?

In the app: Profile tab → gear icon → Settings → Delete account. Everything server-side is removed within 30 days.

Which Apple Health data does it read?

Active energy, resting energy, steps, weight, body fat and workouts — read-only, never written back.
